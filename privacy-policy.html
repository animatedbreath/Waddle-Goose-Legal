Privacy Policy

Waddle Goose · Effective 29 September 2026

Waddle Goose does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising, and it never connects to the internet.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no login, no profile, and no way to enter personal information. It does not read your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or device identifiers. It contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no attribution or tracking SDK of any kind.

What is stored on your device

A few small files are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPad, so the game remembers things between sessions:

  • Settings — the language you chose, the music and sound volumes, and whether the short how-to has already been shown.
  • Play counters — totals such as rounds played, rounds won, food eaten and hops, used only to unlock dress-up items. They belong to the iPad, not to a person: there are no player profiles, and they are not tied to anyone's name or identity.
  • Outfits — what each of the four seats' geese is wearing.

None of this ever leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Network use

None. The game makes no network requests of any kind and contains no links to websites or other apps. It is fully playable with the iPad in Airplane Mode.

Children

Waddle Goose is designed to be played by children alongside adults. Because we collect no data at all, we collect no data from children. There is nothing to purchase inside the app, no advertising of any kind, and no way to contact anyone from within it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
